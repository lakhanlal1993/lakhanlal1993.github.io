Privacy

Simple, clear choices.

This static portfolio does not use analytics unless its owner adds a Google Analytics measurement ID in site-config.js.

When a measurement ID is enabled, Google Analytics remains off until a visitor chooses “Allow analytics.” The choice is stored in the visitor's browser. Use “Privacy settings” in the page footer to change it later. Declining analytics does not block any page content.

Google may process technical visit information under its own policies. The site does not send contact details or CV information to Analytics.
